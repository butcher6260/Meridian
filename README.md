## 🛠 Features

The application provides a suite of tools for daily productivity.

### Focus Sessions
Use the timer to manage depth of work. The app tracks your pomodoro intervals, helping you balance short work sprints with breaks. This prevents fatigue and keeps your mind sharp throughout the day. 

### Countdown Timers
Set custom countdowns for deadlines or meetings. The visual indicator keeps you aware of time limits. You can create multiple timers for different projects. 

### Scheduling
Input your tasks into the schedule view. The app sorts these by time, allowing you to see your full day in one list. You can update your plans as priorities change.

### Menu Bar Companion
The companion gives you constant access to your timers. It stays in your system tray, taking up minimal space while offering full control over your active sessions.

### Widgets
Place widgets on your home screen to track progress without opening the full application window. See how much time remains on your current timer or read your next task directly from your desktop.

### How do I update the application?
When a new version releases, visit the download link again. Download the latest file, run it, and the installer will replace your current version with the new one. Your settings usually transfer automatically.

### Does the app run while closed?
The application stays active in the background. It continues to count down even if you minimize the main window. This ensures you never miss a timer alert.

### Is the app secure?
The application runs locally on your machine. Your data, such as tasks and timers, remains on your hard drive. No sensitive information leaves your device.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues during installation or usage, try these solutions.

*   **Installer fails to run:** Ensure your user account has administrative privileges. You may need to click 'Run as Administrator' when opening the installer file.
*   **App crashes on launch:** Check if you have the latest updates for your operating system. Restarting your computer often fixes small conflicts.
*   **Widgets not appearing:** Right-click your desktop and ensure widgets are enabled in your system settings.
*   **Settings not saving:** Ensure you have enough disk space available for the application cache.
